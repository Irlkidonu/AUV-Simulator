# Navigation Integration Extension

The following files constitute the complete optional extension. They are
independent of the simulator's baseline runtime and may be reviewed, committed,
or reverted as one unit.

- `extensions/navigation_integration/README.md`
- `extensions/navigation_integration/uuv_navigation_extension/__init__.py`
- `extensions/navigation_integration/uuv_navigation_extension/dvl_semantics.py`
- `extensions/navigation_integration/uuv_navigation_extension/fiducial_geometry.py`
- `extensions/navigation_integration/test/test_dvl_semantics.py`
- `extensions/navigation_integration/test/test_fiducial_geometry.py`
- `NAVIGATION_INTEGRATION_EXTENSION.md`

Scientific evidence and results do not belong in this reusable extension.
